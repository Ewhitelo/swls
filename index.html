What shapes life satisfaction

There is no single key to life satisfaction. It is a recipe with several ingredients. Most people can name the ones that matter to them, though temperament, a general tendency to be happy or unhappy, can color the answers.

Relationships

Close, supportive family and friends are among the strongest influences. Losing someone close can lower satisfaction, and recovery can take time.

Work and roles

School, paid or unpaid work, or a role like caregiver. Meaningful work that fits your strengths helps. Stalled progress on important goals hurts.

Personal life

Satisfaction with yourself, spiritual or religious life, learning and growth, leisure, and health. Frustration in these areas can be a strong source of dissatisfaction.

Questions to think about

  1. Which of the five statements did you rate highest? Which lowest? What explains the gap?
  2. Was your score shaped by something recent, or has it felt this way for a long time?
  3. Which area of life (relationships, work or school, personal growth, health, leisure) contributes most to your answers?
  4. Is there one change, large or small, that would move one of your ratings up by a point?

This is a reflection tool, not a diagnosis.
